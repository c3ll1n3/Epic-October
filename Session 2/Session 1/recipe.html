THE RECIPE

Homemade french fries

Do you love golden, crispy fries? This simple recipe shows you how to prepare delicious homemade french fries using a few everyday ingredients. They are perfect as a snack or as a side dish.

Ingredients

  • 5 medium-sized potatoes
  • 3 cups of cooking oil
  • salt
  • cold water for soaking

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes thoroughly and peel them
  2. Cut the potatoes into thin evenly sized strips
  3. soak the strips in cold water for 30 minutes to remove some surface starch
  4. Drain the water and dry the potatoes completely using a kitchen towel
  5. Heat the oil in a suitable pot.Carefully fry the potatoes in small batches until golden and crispy. Keep them away fromsplashing oil
  6. sprinkle with salt and serve while hot
